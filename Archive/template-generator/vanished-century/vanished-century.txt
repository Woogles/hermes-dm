# Vanished Century Lore

The party are agents of the Midnight Circle, a young investigator organization investigating impossible cases.

Something has been stealing people, objects, and memories from ordinary places while leaving clean, impossible holes behind.

## World layers

### Aboveworld

Normal modern society:

- Cities
- Schools
- Museums
- Train stations
- Suburbs
- News networks

The party begins here.

### Ring of Stones

A hidden ley-line network beneath old cities.

### Underhollow

A magical underground city built in stone, root, and crystal.

## Core rule

The campaign is about proving what happened, not assuming what anyone remembers.

Physical evidence, written records, witness memories, and magical traces may disagree.

The party must identify:

- physical facts
- corrected records
- witness contradictions
- magical echoes
- anchors

# Session 0: The Vanished Century

## Character guidance

Keep characters simple.

Recommended beginner classes:

- Fighter
- Barbarian
- Ranger
- Rogue
- Cleric
- Druid
- Wizard with prepared spell lists
- Bard
- Paladin
- Sorcerer

Give each player:

- One favorite skill
- One trusted spell or feature
- One background:
  - Student
  - Investigator
  - City child
  - Forest guide

## Opening hook

Read to players:

> “The Midnight Circle received a strange report. Three witnesses say a person was missing from a photo for years. The photo changes when looked at from memory, but not when looked at physically. No theft was reported. No damage was reported. One witness says the person remembers being saved, but the records say the person was never saved. Something in the past is wrong.”

Then ask:

- What do you investigate first?
- What do you ask witnesses?
- Do you trust memory, records, or physical proof?
