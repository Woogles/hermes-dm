# The Salt-Tide Archive Lore

The coastal town of Saltmouth keeps records of tides, floods, lighthouse memories, and harbor names.

But some people remember a flood that no record shows. Some records say the flood happened but no one admits it. Some tide logs contradict themselves.

Physical water evidence is real. Memory can be true, false, copied, drowned, or made to be believed.

# Session 0: The Salt-Tide Archive

## Characters

Keep it simple.

Recommended classes:

- Fighter
- Ranger
- Rogue
- Bard
- Cleric
- Druid
- Wizard with prepared spells

Give each player:

- One favorite skill
- One trusted spell or feature
- One background:
  - Tide child
  - Harbor clerk
  - Lighthouse apprentice
  - Salt runner

## Opening hook

Read to players:

> “The tide archive says there was a flood in one year. The town council says there was not. The pavement says it was dry. A witness says she saw water. Which one should you check first?”

Then ask:

- What do you check first?
- Do you trust the record, the witness, or the pavement?
- What can you prove?
