# Session 0: The Salt-Tide Archive

## Characters

Keep it simple.

Recommended classes:

- Fighter
- Ranger
- Rogue
- Bard
- Cleric
- Druid
- Wizard with prepared spells

Give each player:

- One favorite skill
- One trusted spell or feature
- One background:
  - Tide child
  - Harbor clerk
  - Lighthouse apprentice
  - Salt runner

## Opening hook

Read to players:

> “The tide archive says there was a flood in one year. The town council says there was not. The pavement says it was dry. A witness says she saw water. Which one should you check first?”

Then ask:

- What do you check first?
- Do you trust the record, the witness, or the pavement?
- What can you prove?
