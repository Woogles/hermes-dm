# Vanished Century Lore

The party are agents of the **Midnight Circle**, a young investigator organization investigating impossible cases.

The campaign is mystery-first and investigation-first. Combat exists, but it is never the main solution.

## Premise

Something has been stealing people, objects, and memories from ordinary modern places, but leaving clean, impossible holes behind.

The campaign is about proving what happened, not assuming what anyone remembers.

## World layers

### Aboveworld

Normal modern society:

- Cities
- Schools
- Museums
- Train stations
- Suburbs
- News networks

The party begins here.

### Ring of Stones

A hidden ley-line network beneath old cities:

- Forgotten tunnels
- Old libraries
- Hidden parks
- Buried statues
- Subway passages that do not match city maps

This layer becomes available after Act 1.

### Underhollow

A magical underground city:

- Built in stone, root, and crystal
- Home to fairy society
- Old goblin gangs
- Giant troll security
- Secret police
- A council of ancient beings

The Underhollow is the campaign’s hidden magical world layer.

## The party’s role

The party are:

- Young investigators
- Students of strange facts
- People who respect evidence
- People who notice when memory does not match record
- People who can work with magical witnesses

They are not expected to be perfect combat experts.

## Key campaign rule: Case Marks

Instead of complex time magic, the campaign uses Case Marks.

Case Marks:

- Spend one Case Mark to re-examine a clue.
- Compare two records.
- Ask a witness how they remember an event.
- Notice a magical echo.
- Reconstruct a broken sequence of events.
- The DM tells the player one true detail that matters.

The party starts each session with:

- 3 Case Marks if level 1-4
- 4 Case Marks if level 5-8
- 5 Case Marks if level 9-12

## Beginner skill DCs

Keep DCs simple:

- Simple clue: DC 10-12
- Clear contradiction: DC 15
- Hidden magical cause: DC 18
- Deep memory or paradox: DC 20-23

## Beginner-friendly design rules

- Give at least one obvious clue every scene.
- Ask one question at a time.
- If a check fails, reveal something useful.
- Do not use complex lairs early.
- One major threat and one minor threat per combat encounter.
- Give players a story option before combat.
- Allow Insight checks to learn tactics.
- If combat fails badly, create a consequence, not instant death.

## Paradox track

Use a simple paradox track:

- 0 = clean
- 1 = small contradiction
- 2 = faction reacts
- 3 = public danger
- 4 = major paradox
- 5 = catastrophic

## Beginner mistakes to avoid

Do not:

- Make the mystery too vague
- Use too many hidden clues
- Make every failure severe
- Make players use time magic constantly
- Make combat the main solution
- Make players choose every branch at once

Do instead:

- Give one obvious clue
- Ask one simple question
- Show one contradiction
- Offer one clear choice
- Let success matter, not just success over failure
