paper-circus/lore.md

# The Paper Circus Lore

The town of Vellam remembers a big circus parade every year, but some people say one ringmaster, one performer, or one float was never part of it.

The campaign is about memory and proof.

Posters may be real.
Tickets may be false.
A witness may remember too perfectly.
An archive may have copied one version of the show.

# Session 0: The Paper Circus

## Characters

Keep it simple.

Recommended classes:

- Fighter
- Ranger
- Rogue
- Bard
- Cleric
- Druid
- Wizard with prepared spells

Give each player:

- One favorite skill
- One trusted spell or feature
- One background:
  - Parade child
  - Ticket seller
  - Town apprentice
  - Lantern runner

## Opening hook

Read to players:

> “Every year, Vellam remembers a big parade. But one year, a ticket says one show happened. The archive says another show happened. A child remembers a float that no one else can name. What do you trust first: the ticket, the archive, or the child's memory?”

  id: missing-ringmaster
  act: 1
  level_range: "1-2"
  objective: |
    Discover why the town remembers a ringmaster who does not appear in the ledger.
  major_clues:
    - poster with ringmaster
    - ticket says one show
    - witness remembers too perfectly
  success_condition: identify ringmaster memory
  failure_condition: paradox +1
  skill_challenge:
    - check: Investigation
      dc: 10
      meaning: find poster and ticket
    - check: History
      dc: 10
      meaning: compare memory to ledger
    - check: Insight
      dc: 12
      meaning: witness memory is too perfect
