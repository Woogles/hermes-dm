# Session 0: A Shattered Century

## Character guidance

This campaign is advanced. Keep combat normal, but emphasize planning, travel choices, anchors, and paradox consequences.

## Opening hook

Read to players:

> “Harlowe has a drought, but the town archives say it rained in the past. Worse, the market’s layout changes from year to year. A clockwork device beneath the Clockwork Vaults has three broken anchors. If the device stays damaged, Harlowe will not be one town. It will be four towns trying to become one.”

Then ask:

- What do you investigate first?
- Do you trust records, physical traces, or memory?
- Which era do you visit?
- Which anchor will you use?
