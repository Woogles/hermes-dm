Brackenmere Square: Meeting place, notice board, council hall, and the first site of the bell’s unexplained ringing.
The Dry Lakebed: Mudflats, stranded boats, exposed stone markers, and dangerous sinkholes.
The Reedcutters’ Wharf: A working dock on the lake’s remaining water. The guild can provide boats, ropes, and local knowledge.
The Sunken Causeway: A stone road leading toward the buried tower. Its carved markers show a sequence of village events.
The Bell Tower Below: A half-flooded ruin. Its lower chamber contains the bell and the presence bound to it.
