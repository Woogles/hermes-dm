Village gratitude: Free lodging and modest supplies after the first adventure.
Guild’s lake map: Reveals a safe route across the exposed lakebed.
Lantern of Clear Recall: Once per long rest, its bearer can help a nearby creature recover a memory recently obscured by magic or supernatural effects.
Ending reward: The village may restore the bell tower as a memorial, dismantle it, or preserve it as a guarded site. The party’s choice affects Brackenmere’s trust and future prosperity.
