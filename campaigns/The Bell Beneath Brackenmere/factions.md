Brackenmere Council — Wants the village safe and its reputation intact. Starts neutral toward the party. Trust rises if the party shares evidence and protects residents.
Reedcutters’ Guild — Local workers who know the lakebed and its hidden paths. Starts friendly if treated respectfully. They distrust outsiders who damage the causeway.
The Lantern Keepers — A small circle that maintains lights along the old road. They believe the bell should remain silent, but conceal that their founder helped seal the tower.
